# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We want this project to be a respectful, focused, and useful place to collaborate. Participants are expected to communicate with patience, assume good intent, and keep technical discussion grounded in evidence.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and direct.
- Give actionable feedback.
- Accept disagreement without personal attacks.
- Avoid publishing private data, credentials, screenshots with sensitive identifiers, or logs that include secrets.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, or personal insults.
- Sharing another person's private information.
- Posting secrets, tokens, credentials, or private operational data.
- Repeated disruptive behavior after maintainers ask for it to stop.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments and may restrict participation when needed to keep the project safe and useful.
